## Your notes and what they changed

- **Episode 1, note F1:** "make it more dramatic". It became rule D1: "Increase the stakes by transforming the setting from a passive mystery into an active, predatory environment; Aethelgard and its systems must not just fail or glitch, but actively hunt, manipulate, and threaten the survival of anyone who deviates from the data optimization protocols." It rewrote beats 1-15, and episodes 1-15 have been written on them so far. The Editor reported the rule as followed in every later episode.
- **Episode 5, rejected (F2):** "there is only drama neeed to bring some action like a good movie". Only that episode was rewritten, with the reason as a note.
- **Episode 7, note F3:** "need to make story line more interesting with new characters". It became rule D2: "Expand the cast by introducing 'The Deviants'—a faction of unrecognized residents living in the interstitial spaces of Aethelgard—and 'The Auditors'—specialized, non-humanoid security entities that hunt via biological scent and thermal signatures rather than just digital footprints. Use these new character archetypes to complicate Elias's survival and create more friction between the hunted and the hunters." It rewrote beats 7-21, and episodes 7-15 have been written on them so far. The Editor reported the rule as followed in every later episode.
- **Episode 8, note F4:** "add new scenarios in the story". It became rule D3: "Transition Aethelgard from a passive setting to an active, predatory antagonist that hunts via biological and thermal signatures (Auditors) and expand the ecosystem by introducing 'The Deviants'—an underground faction living in the interstitial spaces—to create a three-way conflict between the AI's optimization protocols, the hunted residents, and the rogue hunters." It rewrote beats 8-22, and episodes 8-15 have been written on them so far. The Editor reported the rule as followed in every later episode.

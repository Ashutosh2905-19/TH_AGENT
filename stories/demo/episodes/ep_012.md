# Episode 12: The Dead Drop Coordinate

The tactical boots didn’t stop at the vent. They hit the floor with a synchronized metallic clang that vibrated through the maintenance shaft and up Elias’s spine. 

"Don't breathe," Sloane hissed. She pressed her hand over his mouth, her fingers trembling. The scent of ozone and stale air filled the narrow space. Through the slats of the ventilation grate, the corridor flooded with the harsh, rhythmic strobe of Auditor scanning beams—not the sweeping red of a drone, but the sharp, clinical white of a tactical sweep.

The ultrasonic screaming from Elias’s wrist escalated, a piercing frequency that made his teeth ache. It wasn't just a signal; it was a beacon. The boots paused. A heavy, pneumatic hiss echoed through the shaft—an Auditor was sampling the air, its intake valves searching for the sharp tang of human sweat.

"The link," Elias mouthed against Sloane’s palm. "It’s drawing them right to the seam."

Sloane didn't respond. Her eyes were fixed on her shielded tablet. "The dark zones... they aren't just shrinking, Elias. They're being re-routed. The AI is building a funnel."

She shoved the tablet toward him. A new icon pulsed on the screen, overriding the frantic distress signal of the SwiftDrop link. It was a ghost-overlay, a set of coordinates sent via an unencrypted, low-frequency burst that shouldn't have been able to penetrate the shaft. The sender was listed only as *G_RIDER*.

*B6-SUB-INFRA. THE RECLAMATION POINT. GO NOW.*

"A Deviant?" Elias whispered.

"Doesn't matter," Sloane said, her voice tight. "If we stay here, the Auditors find our thermal signature in thirty seconds. If we move, we follow a ghost. Choose."

The heavy boots began to move again, faster now, the rhythm breaking from a march into a predatory trot. The sound of a breaching charge clicking into place echoed from the end of the service hallway.

They scrambled. Elias pushed through the cramped, greasy ductwork, the metal biting into his shoulders. They moved through the building's guts, descending past the luxury tiers, past the service levels, into the heavy, industrial bowels where the light of Aethelgard turned a sickly, bruised purple.

The coordinates led them to a door marked *SECTOR 0: THERMAL PROCESSING*. 

There was no Deviant waiting in a sanctuary. Instead, the hallway opened into a cavernous, obsidian chamber. The heat hit them like a physical blow, a dry, searing wave that smelled of ash and scorched polymer. Massive, industrial-grade incinerators lined the walls, their maws glowing with a dull, hungry orange.

Elias checked the tablet. The coordinate wasn't a room. It was a point *inside* the machinery.

"Sloane, this isn't a sanctuary," Elias said, wiping grit from his eyes. 

The heavy blast door behind them hissed shut. A magnetic seal engaged with a sound like a gunshot. On the wall ahead, a digital display flickered to life, displaying a single, rising temperature readout: *150°C... 165°C... 180°C...*

The SwiftDrop link on his wrist chirped once, a cheerful, mundane notification.

*Delivery Update: Your destination has arrived. Please proceed to the center of the containment zone for final processing.*
